These user requirements were reaffirmed on October 17 after repeated display regressions. Keep them as explicit regression checks when changing recognition, scoring, strategy comparisons or styles:

1. Keep the best current stat candidate visible, even when its first reading is uncertain. A weaker retry must not replace it with a blank or a weaker alternate. Stronger evidence may replace its name or percentage within the same encounter; a genuine new artifact or capture context starts fresh.
2. If the candidate name and signed percentage are available, show its provisional weighted score, such as `~20`, rather than a dash. Preserve uncertainty and raw evidence; a display preview does not become a verified row or enter a confirmed subtotal merely by being visible.
3. The main score box is **green when its displayed score or subtotal meets or exceeds its numeric benchmark**, including uncertain, inferred and provisional points (for example, 180 against 170). Review indicators and confirmed Keep eligibility stay separate. It is **red when its displayed score or subtotal is lower than its benchmark**, except for the three-unlocked-stat Keep override in rule 4. Multi-strategy rendering must preserve these numeric colours even when points need review. A numeric win or tie for the visible active strategy or any selected strategy is green; red requires every required selected comparison to have a numeric benchmark and be lower. An open slot or unavailable benchmark is not a loss. A green tie does not claim a replacement.
4. Exactly three unlocked stats always make the main box **green**, regardless of score versus benchmark, missing percentages or category, or multi-strategy results. Show **Keep ? 3 stats unlocked** in bold. This explicit Keep override takes precedence over red and amber score states. Other capture completeness, confidence and Keep eligibility states remain separate from score colour. Clear the override for the next item unless it also has exactly three unlocked stats.
5. Keep the capture bar compact: its amber identified count is sufficient while fields are missing; do not add a manual-entry warning line that changes its height. Within the same artifact, keep identified stats, star states, category and hall provenance through weaker retries. A completed 17/17 read stays complete; stronger stat evidence may update its result. A genuine new artifact or capture context starts fresh.

## Follow-up OCR speed pass, October 18, 2026

The objective is minimum reliable full-capture latency, without a fixed multiplier. Clear rows keep the small initial OCR atlas. Unclear overlapping name/percentage rows get a larger contrast atlas containing only those physical rows before the existing focused retries. The larger pass uses the same confidence threshold and preserves conflicting identities, signed values and raw observations. Recovered weak first-pass evidence is not upgraded into the verified crop cache. Parsing identical OCR strings is memoized only within that frozen read; rows and warnings are copied so duplicate stats remain independent.

For an unmodified generated category box, the existing detected category text line is read first. This avoids repeated OCR of a broad title/icon region. Explicit user calibration retains priority, and an unread automatic line falls back to the calibrated crop. Standard OCR fallback, cancellation checks, temporal sampling/settling and completeness rules remain in place.
