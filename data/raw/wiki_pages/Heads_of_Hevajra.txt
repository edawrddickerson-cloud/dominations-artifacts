== Historical Description ==
The Heads of Hevajra is a symbolic artifact associated with the Buddhist deity Hevajra, often depicted with multiple heads. It represents the transformation of negative mental states, such as attachment and ignorance, and the journey toward enlightenment. Each head is seen as embodying a different aspect of the cycle of suffering, and through this symbolism, it points to the possibility of overcoming these challenges. The artifact is used in ritual practices to help deepen meditation and understanding of wisdom, impermanence, and the interconnectedness of all things.
