'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is one of The [[Mythical Artifacts]], that can be stored and displayed at [[Museum]].

== Historical Description ==
The Fire Breathing Bunny Fossil is a whimsical artifact discovered in a Welsh cave, believed to be the remains of a legendary medieval rabbit said to breathe fire and terrify knights. Its charred bones and sharp teeth tell the tale of a beast so fierce it took a divine explosive to defeat it. While skeptics dismiss it as a hoax, the fossil remains a playful and beloved piece of folklore, proving that history can be as strange as it is entertaining.
