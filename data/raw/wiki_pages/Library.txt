==General Information==
*The '''Library''' is an [[Buildings|Economic Building]] where you research various [[Technology|Technologies]] to help improve your troops and town's defense and economy in ''[[DomiNations]]''.
*The [[Technology|Technologies]] are organized into three categories and in a specific color: brown represents economy, blue represents defense, and red represents military.
*The '''Library''' is unlocked at [[Classical Age]].
*It takes four citizens to construct and upgrade the '''Library'''.
*Each [[Technology]] has five chapters.
*The Library is divided into four Halls:  Through the Ages, Archeology, League of Nations, and State of Defense.

==Trivia==
*The '''Library''' is based off the [http://riseofnations.wikia.com/wiki/Library Library] in Rise of Nations, a game devoloped in 2004 by Big Huge Games; the same creator of ''[[DomiNations]]''.
*Originally, the '''Library's''' [[Technology|Technologies]] were organized into five categories, based off the categories of the '''Library''' in Rise of Nations. These categories according to the colors were Brown which represents Age, Red which represents military, Blue which represents civics, Green which represents commerce, and Yellow which represents Science. Over time, the amount of categories decreased from five to the official four in ''[[DomiNations]]''.
*In early stages of ''[[DomiNations]]'', each [[Technology]] had three chapters. It later increased to four chapters in early 2016 and five chapters in mid-2016.
