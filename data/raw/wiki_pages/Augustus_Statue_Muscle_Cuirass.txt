== General Description ==

'''Augustus Statue Muscle Cuirass''' is one of The [[Legendary Artifacts]], that can be stored and displayed at [[Museum]]. It is purchased from limited time sale. Cost 100 token

== Historical Description ==

The Augustus of Prima Porta is a marble statue of the first Roman emperor that was found in a Villa near Rome. In its center, Augustus accepts a battle standard from a foreign ruler. This refers to his greatest diplomatic achievement: negotiating the return of the legionary eagles lost to Parthia at the Battle of Carrhae. Surrounding the emperor in has-relief are gods that symbolize his peaceful reign to come, as well as figures representing various subjugated lands and peoples.
