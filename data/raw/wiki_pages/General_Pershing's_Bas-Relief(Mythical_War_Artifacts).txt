== Historical Description ==
General John Joseph 'Black Jack' Pershing was a senior United States Army Officer during World War I. He served as the Commander of the American Expeditionary Forces on the Western Front from 1917-1918. General Pershing is the only American in history to earn the rank of General of the Armies during his lifetime (George Washington was posthumously given the rank). this Bas-Relief was created by sculptor Walter Hancock in 1935 for the Liberty Memorial in Kansas City.
