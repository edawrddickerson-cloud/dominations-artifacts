How the comparison works

Sums all five stats at maximum percentages, including reroll potential, and compares against your second-best owned item of the same type and hall in this profile, including Unused. Main Weapons follow the page's Off/Def toggles; Jewelry uses Off, Armor and Pottery use Def, and War items use Off. Each category's cutoff uses the same ratings as its item score. An unread Main type shows the highest possible total across these rating choices. Ties do not count as better.
