Crafting Scanner

Observed sample frequencies

Crafts recorded
Contributed encounters, including incomplete and unkept records; cleared records excluded.
Starting rows at 11%
Signed starting rolls with magnitude 11 ÷ eligible starting rows whose reviewed range includes magnitude 11.
Artifacts with ≥1 starting 11%
Eligible artifacts with at least one starting magnitude-11 roll ÷ eligible artifacts with at least one row whose range includes magnitude 11.
Artifacts with ≥2 starting 11%
Eligible artifacts with at least two starting magnitude-11 rolls ÷ the same eligible artifact denominator.
Repeated canonical perk
Eligible artifacts with a canonical perk in at least two positions ÷ artifacts with five eligible starting rows.
Eligible artifact
Live capture; observed panel transition; independently read Sell for and Continue controls; five complete, conflict-free starting rows; reviewed signed ranges. Initial captures, ambiguous identity, unknown ranges, inferred values and upgraded rows excluded.
Main ordinary starting range
Magnitudes 1, 6, 11; fixed canonical sign.
War ordinary starting range
Magnitudes 1, 3, 6; no 11% denominator.
Other ranges
Excluded until independently reviewed. Current upgraded values never count as starting rolls.
95% CI
Wilson sample intervals; row intervals assume independent rows.
Unknown context
No verified dataset match for crafting event or game version.
Reporting threshold
At least five contributing accounts per filtered sample.
Week
Monday 00:00 UTC through now. Undated legacy totals excluded.

Sampling limits

  • Opt-in scanner sample; missed crafts, revisits and OCR errors remain possible.
  • Encounter transitions do not prove a new craft. Separate identical encounters retain separate IDs.
  • Excluded incomplete reads and unsupported ranges can bias frequencies.
  • Rows within an artifact and crafts from one account can be correlated.
  • Confidence intervals exclude selection bias and recognition error.
  • Observed frequencies are not established game probabilities.
