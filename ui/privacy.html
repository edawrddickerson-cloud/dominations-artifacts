Privacy and your data

Policy version: 18 October 2026. Manage account and sharing preferences.

Local use

Scanning and scoring run in your browser. Collections, strategies, museum layouts, settings, scanner logs and cropped artwork are saved on this device. Clearing browser site data can remove them. Export backups from Profiles and keep them somewhere safe.

Optional account storage

When accounts are enabled, you sign in through Discord using Supabase Auth. Discord and Supabase process the basic account identity used for sign-in; this may include your Discord name, identifier, avatar metadata and email address. The planner uses the Supabase account ID to keep your saved data together across devices. Linking this browser to an account synchronizes profiles, collections, strategies, museum layouts, Council and scoring settings. Captured artwork, raw screenshots, scanner logs, device calibration and authentication tokens are excluded from cloud workspace documents. Signing out leaves local planner data on this device; consider this when using a shared computer.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

These purposes have separate, initially disabled preferences. Product analytics sends explicit feature actions, a pseudonymous reference and meaningful activity duration to PostHog. Diagnostics sends structured error locations and sampled processing/reliability metrics. Context is limited to the application version, page, browser family, broad device type and viewport size band. Full OCR text, screenshots, custom strategy names, email addresses and sign-in URLs are excluded. Session replay and broad click autocapture are not used.

Analytics for signed-in users uses an opaque account reference; local-only visitors use a separate pseudonymous reference. Your current analytics reference is available in Account & privacy for access or deletion requests. Metrics describe people who choose to share and may be incomplete when collection is blocked or offline.

Analytics and diagnostics are processed in PostHog Cloud EU on the Free plan, with a one-year event analysis window. That window limits the history available for analysis; it is not a guarantee that data is physically erased at its end. Deletion requests are handled separately and provider processing can take longer. See PostHog event retention.

Optional crafting research

Research contributions are not available in this release. When available, research sharing is separate from account sync and analytics. You must enable it and explicitly start a research session. A scanner encounter is not a crafting outcome. Only results explicitly confirmed as new crafts are contributed. Records include ordered perk evidence, source and correction flags, timestamps, completeness, optional user-reported game/event context and explicit retained/rejected decisions. Contribution records remain linked privately to your account so they can be exported or deleted. They are not anonymous while this link exists, and raw contributions are not publicly accessible.

Your choices and recovery

Optional sharing can be withdrawn in Account & privacy without affecting core features. Withdrawal stops new optional collection and clears pending local research for the active account. Previously contributed research can be deleted separately. Account export includes cloud workspace data and contributed research; local artwork and logs are included in the separate planner backup. Account deletion removes active cloud account data and requests deletion of account-linked analytics; provider processing and backup expiry may take longer.

Before replacing a local or cloud workspace, the app retains safety copies on this device. These may contain the previous local artwork and data. The three most recent copies can be downloaded from Account & privacy. Cloud workspace history retains up to three prior versions and is pruned on later saves. These recovery copies supplement independent backups.

Hosting, retention and contact

For privacy, data-access or deletion requests, contact ededed84@gmail.com. Core site hosting and account services may retain operational security logs needed to deliver the service. Availability and published retention for this version are shown in Account & privacy. Recovery backups have separate expiry arrangements. Previously retained operational backups may expire later than active account records.

When optional sharing is unavailable, its controls are disabled. No optional sharing is required to use the planner.
