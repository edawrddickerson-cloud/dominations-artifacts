Privacy and your data

Policy version: 18 October 2026. Manage account and sharing preferences.

Local use

Scanning and scoring run in your browser. Collections, strategies, museum layouts, settings, scanner logs and cropped artwork are saved on this device. Clearing browser site data can remove them. Export backups from Profiles and keep them somewhere safe.

Optional account storage

When accounts are enabled, you sign in through Discord using Supabase Auth. Discord and Supabase process the basic account identity used for sign-in; this may include your Discord name, identifier, avatar metadata and email address. The planner uses the Supabase account ID to keep your saved data together across devices. Linking this browser to an account synchronizes profiles, collections, strategies, museum layouts, Council and scoring settings. V2 backs up structured crafting history and lifetime counter records with your private workspace, independently of optional sharing. Captured artwork, raw OCR, diagnostic scans, device calibration and authentication tokens remain excluded. Signing out leaves local planner data on this device; consider this when using a shared computer.

Optional analytics and diagnostics

These purposes have separate, initially disabled preferences. Product analytics sends explicit feature actions, a pseudonymous reference and meaningful activity duration to PostHog. Diagnostics sends structured error locations and sampled processing/reliability metrics. Context is limited to the application version, page, browser family, broad device type and viewport size band. Full OCR text, screenshots, custom strategy names, email addresses and sign-in URLs are excluded. Session replay and broad click autocapture are not used.

Analytics for signed-in users uses an opaque account reference; local-only visitors use a separate pseudonymous reference. Your current analytics reference is available in Account & privacy for access or deletion requests. Metrics describe people who choose to share and may be incomplete when collection is blocked or offline.

Analytics and diagnostics are processed in PostHog Cloud EU on the Free plan, with a one-year event analysis window. That window limits the history available for analysis; it is not a guarantee that data is physically erased at its end. Deletion requests are handled separately and provider processing can take longer. See PostHog event retention.

Optional crafting research

Live contributions remain disabled. V2 requires separate opt-in. Structured evidence comes from the automatic log, including unkept and incomplete encounters. No images, raw OCR, Discord names, profile names or inventory decisions. Account ownership remains private for corrections, exports and deletion. Reports contain aggregates only, with at least five contributing accounts per filtered sample. Evidence expires 730 days after observation. Withdrawal deletes contributions when connected; minimal consent revocation receipts remain until account deletion. Methods and sampling limits.

Optional OCR feedback

Not available in this release. Separate opt-in and sign-in required. Samples contain individual unrecognized OCR labels, suggested matches, confidence, user corrections, hall, artifact category and scanner build. No images or full logs. Samples stay account-linked in Supabase, with no public access. Account export and deletion include these samples; OCR feedback can also be deleted separately. Active samples are pruned after 90 days without updates. Matching changes require review.

Your choices and recovery

Optional sharing can be withdrawn in Account & privacy without affecting core features. Withdrawal stops new optional collection and clears pending local research for the active account. V2 withdrawal deletes community contributions on reconnect. Private account history remains backed up. Account export includes cloud workspace data and contributed research; local artwork and logs are included in the separate planner backup. Account deletion removes active cloud account data and requests deletion of account-linked analytics; provider processing and backup expiry may take longer.

Before replacing a local or cloud workspace, the app retains safety copies on this device. These may contain the previous local artwork and data. The three most recent copies can be downloaded from Account & privacy. Cloud workspace history retains up to three prior versions and is pruned on later saves. These recovery copies supplement independent backups.

Availability and published retention for this version are shown in Account & privacy. Recovery backups have separate expiry arrangements. When optional sharing is unavailable, its controls are disabled. No optional sharing is required to use the planner.
